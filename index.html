This Privacy Policy explains how PropertyConnect-AI collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information when you use our property enquiry, property matching, website and WhatsApp-related services.

1. Introduction

PropertyConnect-AI ("PropertyConnect-AI", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

By using PropertyConnect-AI or providing information to us, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Depending on how you interact with PropertyConnect-AI, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Mobile telephone or WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • Property enquiry details
  • Preferred property location and property type
  • Property size requirements
  • Purchase or rental budget
  • Buyer, tenant, seller or landlord requirements
  • Property details submitted for sale or rental
  • Preferred viewing dates and times
  • Messages sent through WhatsApp or other communication channels
  • Information voluntarily provided during enquiries
  • Records relating to property enquiries and property matching
  • Technical information generated when using our website or online services

3. How We Collect Information

We may collect information when you:

  • Contact PropertyConnect-AI through WhatsApp
  • Submit a property enquiry
  • Submit property details for sale or rental
  • Request property information
  • Request a property viewing
  • Respond to PropertyConnect-AI automated messages
  • Complete an online form
  • Communicate with our property representatives
  • Use our website or related digital services

4. How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to property enquiries
  • Identify properties matching your requirements
  • Match prospective buyers or tenants with available properties
  • Match sellers or landlords with prospective customers
  • Provide property information
  • Arrange property viewings
  • Contact you about your enquiry
  • Maintain property enquiry records
  • Avoid duplicate enquiries
  • Follow up on property enquiries
  • Improve our property matching process and customer service
  • Operate PropertyConnect-AI and its automated functions
  • Maintain the security and reliability of our systems
  • Comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements

Automated property matching may compare information such as location, property type, price range, size and other requirements against available property listings.

PropertyConnect-AI may communicate with users through WhatsApp and the WhatsApp Business Platform. When you contact us through WhatsApp, information such as your telephone number, messages, property requirements and enquiry information may be processed to respond to your request.

PropertyConnect-AI may use automated systems or artificial intelligence to assist with:

  • Understanding property enquiries
  • Categorising customer requirements
  • Extracting relevant property requirements from messages
  • Searching available property records
  • Matching enquiries with suitable properties
  • Preparing automated responses
  • Identifying duplicate enquiries

Automated results are intended to assist the property enquiry process and may be reviewed or corrected where appropriate.
